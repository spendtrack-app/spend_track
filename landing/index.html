Meet your everyday essentials.

A few thoughtful tools to help you see where your money goes, and decide what comes next.

＋01 / TRACK

Keep the little things in view.

Log expenses and income with a category, date, and note. Set up repeating entries for rent, subscriptions, and other regular payments.

Expense & income tracking
◎02 / PLAN

Give every category a limit.

Set monthly budgets by category. See how much you have left, spot when you’re over, and adjust your limits as life changes.

Monthly budget management
◴03 / UNDERSTAND

See the bigger picture.

Explore spending by day, week, month, or year. Category charts, merchant rankings, and period comparisons help patterns become clear.

Spending visualization
≡04 / REFLECT

Find the story in your history.

Search merchants and notes, filter by category or entry type, and edit past entries. Export your transaction history as a CSV when you need it.

Searchable transaction history
